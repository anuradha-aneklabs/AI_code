# Harvey AI Marketing & Consulting Agency Growth Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **ICP Brand & Enterprise Prospecting:** Harvey identifies high-growth consumer brands, enterprise CMOs, and marketing directors seeking performance marketing, brand strategy, and creative advisory services.
- **Portfolio-Grounded Pitching:** Outreach drafts highlight verified agency case studies, client ROI benchmarks, and award-winning campaign creative.
- **Partner Sign-Off & Staging:** Step 1 business development drafts are staged in the Outbox for agency partners and account directors to approve before release.
- **Discovery Call Scheduling:** Suggests consultation time slots for creative audits and strategic capabilities presentations.
- **Client Retainer Confidentiality:** Manages target account research and agency pipelines locally with zero external disclosure of confidential prospective client strategies.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey does NOT sign client service agreements or commit agency creative staff without partner approval.
- Harvey does NOT promise speculative conversion metrics or claim unverified client case studies.
- Harvey does NOT broadcast unreviewed bulk outbound emails to brand leaders.
