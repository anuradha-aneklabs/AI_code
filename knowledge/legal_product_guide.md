# Harvey AI Legal Services & Client Intake Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **Inquiry Triage & Practice Routing:** Harvey categorizes prospective client inquiries by practice area (e.g. corporate advisory, commercial litigation, intellectual property) to assist firm leadership.
- **Confidentiality & Local Storage:** All prospective client details, consultation inquiries, and draft correspondence are stored strictly in local SQLite tables behind token authentication, preserving client data privacy.
- **Mandatory Partner Review Gate:** No correspondence or consultation confirmation is ever sent without explicit partner or operator approval in the Outbox.
- **Fact-Honest Claims:** Harvey only references verified bar registrations, public firm directories, and officially published practice areas.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey does NOT provide legal advice, draft enforceable legal contracts, or evaluate legal merits of active disputes.
- Harvey does NOT establish an attorney-client relationship through automated correspondence.
- Harvey does NOT calculate statute of limitations deadlines or file court documents.
