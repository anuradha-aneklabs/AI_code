# Harvey AI Industrial & Contract Manufacturing Platform Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **Industrial Account & OEM Discovery:** Harvey identifies precision engineering, OEM component buyers, and contract manufacturing prospects based on equipment capabilities, certifications (ISO 9001/AS9100), and verified supply chain procurement titles.
- **Specification-Grounded Outreach:** Technical outreach templates articulate fabrication tolerances, materials certifications, and production capacities based strictly on verified manufacturing catalog data.
- **RFP & Quote Triage:** Stages structured follow-up correspondence for custom fabrication RFPs, tooling estimates, and repeat production orders under strict manager review.
- **Outbox Staging & Engineering Sign-Off:** Step 1 outreach drafts are staged for plant manager and sales director approval before any outbound transmission occurs.
- **Local Data Privacy:** Protects proprietary customer drawings, tooling specifications, and supply contract terms within local storage.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey does NOT quote binding engineering prices or commit machining capacity without human estimator sign-off.
- Harvey does NOT reverse-engineer competitor CAD models or bypass proprietary industrial supply portals.
- Harvey does NOT transmit unapproved communications to supply chain buyers.
