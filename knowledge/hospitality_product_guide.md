# Harvey AI Hotels & Hospitality Group Sales Platform Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **Corporate Group Booking & Event Qualification:** Harvey identifies corporate event planners, conference organizers, and enterprise travel managers seeking banquet facilities, guest room blocks, and meeting venues.
- **Venue & Amenity Grounding:** Drafts reference confirmed venue square footage, banquet capacities, catering amenities, and seasonal group rates from verified hospitality collateral.
- **Sales Director Outbox Staging:** Staged correspondence for corporate room blocks and banquet inquiries requires explicit approval from the director of sales before delivery.
- **Tour & Site Inspection Scheduling:** Coordinates dates and times for executive site tours and menu tasting consultations.
- **Multi-Property CRM Integration:** Organizes corporate accounts, event dates, and planner preferences in local database records.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey does NOT charge guest credit cards or confirm room reservations without PMS authorization.
- Harvey does NOT override negotiated corporate discount codes or publish internal rack rate margins.
- Harvey does NOT send unapproved emails to event organizers.
