# Harvey AI Commercial HVAC & Facility Services Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **Estimate & Quotation Follow-Up:** Harvey drafts timely, professional follow-up correspondence for commercial HVAC and facility service estimates and RFPs.
- **Service Agreement Renewals:** Coordinates outreach to commercial property managers and building owners approaching planned maintenance contract renewal dates.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Dispatch:** Drafts require operator review before transmission, ensuring service managers maintain total control over customer communications.
- **Local Fleet & Account Logging:** All customer interactions, site locations, and equipment notes remain safely stored in the local SQLite database.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey does NOT perform on-site engineering calculations, equipment sizing, or load calculations.
- Harvey does NOT automatically dispatch technicians to emergency service calls without human dispatcher authorization.
- Harvey does NOT bind the contractor to pricing or warranties without signed written proposals.
