# Harvey AI Freight & Logistics Transportation Platform Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **Commercial Shipper & Lane Discovery:** Harvey identifies manufacturing shippers, wholesale distributors, and 3PL supply chain coordinators needing dry van, refrigerated, or flatbed freight capacity.
- **Lane-Grounded Outreach:** Correspondence references verifiable regional freight corridors, equipment availability, and delivery SLA commitments without fabricating fictitious lane rates.
- **Dispatch & Broker Sign-Off:** All shipper introduction drafts and lane availability updates are placed in the Outbox for logistics broker review prior to transmission.
- **Rate Inquiry Triage:** Flags urgent freight quote requests and spot market inquiries for immediate dispatcher pricing.
- **Secure Carrier Records:** Retains shipper profiles, safety ratings, and contract history in local records.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey does NOT legally bind carriers to bills of lading or execute rate confirmations without dispatcher approval.
- Harvey does NOT track GPS vehicle locations without authorized telematics integration.
- Harvey does NOT send automatic unvetted correspondence to commercial shippers.
