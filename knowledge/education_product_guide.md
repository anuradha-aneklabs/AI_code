# Harvey AI Education & EdTech Admissions Platform Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **Prospective Student & Enterprise Learning Qualification:** Harvey identifies enterprise HR learning directors and prospective candidates seeking accredited professional certifications and higher education degrees.
- **Curriculum-Grounded Correspondence:** Communication sequences reference verified course syllabi, accreditation standards, cohort start dates, and tuition assistance options.
- **Admissions Review Staging:** All inquiry follow-ups and advisory outreach drafts are staged for admissions officer review and approval before sending.
- **Admissions Consultation Scheduling:** Automates appointment scheduling for virtual campus open houses and counselor interviews.
- **Student Privacy Protection:** Strictly preserves applicant privacy in compliance with educational data privacy regulations in local database tables.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey does NOT make binding admissions decisions or approve financial aid grants.
- Harvey does NOT fabricate accreditation credentials or guarantee job placement outcomes.
- Harvey does NOT dispatch unapproved correspondence to applicants or corporate sponsors.
