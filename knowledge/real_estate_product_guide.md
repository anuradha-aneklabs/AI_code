# Harvey AI Commercial & Residential Real Estate Platform Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **Commercial Brokerage & Property Inquiry Qualification:** Harvey automatically identifies high-intent prospective buyers, institutional tenants, and commercial investors across major real estate markets.
- **Listing-Grounded Correspondence:** Outreach and response drafts reference verifiable commercial property data, zoning classifications, square footage, and lease terms without fabricating unverified listings.
- **Broker Sign-Off & Outbox Staging:** Step 1 outreach drafts are staged in the canonical Outbox under `pending_review` status. Licensed brokers retain complete authority to review, edit, or reject drafts before transmission.
- **Viewing & Tour Coordination:** Harvey generates consultation and property walkthrough scheduling suggestions compatible with local calendar standards.
- **Local Data Governance:** Property portfolios, investor contact histories, and brokerage CRM records are stored locally with zero unauthorized external exposure.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey is NOT a licensed real estate broker and does not execute binding lease agreements or title transfers.
- Harvey does NOT bypass MLS authentication barriers or access private off-market deeds without explicit credentials.
- Harvey does NOT send automated messages without operator approval.
