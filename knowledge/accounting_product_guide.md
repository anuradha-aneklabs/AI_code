# Harvey AI Accounting & Advisory Practice Guide

## Verified Capabilities & Features
- **Client Onboarding Coordination:** Harvey drafts structured introductory correspondence and follow-ups for prospective clients seeking audit, tax advisory, or fractional CFO services.
- **Seasonal Intake Workflow:** Assists accounting practices during peak tax and filing seasons by streamlining prospective inquiry responses and consultation scheduling.
- **Operator Review Oversight:** Every communication draft is held in the Outbox with full editability before transmission.
- **Audit-Ready Activity Logging:** Every draft modification, approval, and rejection reason is permanently stored in the audit trail.

## Explicit Limitations & Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Harvey does NOT prepare or file tax returns with IRS or state tax authorities.
- Harvey does NOT render official CPA audit opinions or certify financial statements.
- Harvey does NOT access live banking credentials or execute financial transfers.
